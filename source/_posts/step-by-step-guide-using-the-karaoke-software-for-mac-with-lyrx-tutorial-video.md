## Step-by-Step Guide: Using the Karaoke Software for Mac with LYRX - Tutorial Video

[![LYRX MAC Karaoke Software](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/lyrx-karaokeoverview-coverimage.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/lyrx-karaokeoverview-coverimage.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "LYRX MAC Karaoke Software")

![LYRX karaoke for MAC icon](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/lyrx-appicon.png?resize=80%2C80&ssl=1 "LYRX for MAC Icon")

[LYRX](http://lyrxkaraoke.com/) is modern, clean and nimble **MAC karaoke software** – unlike any other similar product on the market today. The video below provides a quick walk-through of LYRX’s karaoke hosting capabilities, and you can learn more and [download a free trial on the LYRX website](http://lyrxkaraoke.com/free-trial-2/).

LYRX takes many of it’s design cues from [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), PCDJ’s flagship DJ mixing software with years of updates and performance improvements included. **LYRX has been designed to be light on it’s feat and ultra-responsive** – just like a DJ requires when mixing music live.

The LYRX browser system can **easily manage upwards of 800,000 karaoke files**, providing you have the RAM to buffer the tag cache sufficiently. **Search is always instant**, not matter the size of your karaoke music library. **Use search-string style searches** so you can always find just the karaoke track you’re looking for.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Karaoke Software For MAC LYRX](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/MacBook_Pro_lyrx.png?fit=300%2C170&ssl=1 "LYRX Karaoke Software For MAC")](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/MacBook%5FPro%5Flyrx.png?fit=1030%2C583&ssl=1)

LYRX has **native support for MP3+G, Zipped MP3+G and almost all video formats, including the ever-growing in popularity MP4**. Simply access the directory your tracks are in via the finder section of the browser and your track titles will display instantly so you can start hosting and singing without a lengthy and difficult import process.

Sending **‘lyrics only’ out to a second display/monitor** using extended display mode on your MAC is also breeze to setup (showcased in the second video tutorial below).

A basic, and **easy-to-use singers list** is also baked in, allowing you to more effectively **manage your singer rotation** whether you’re at home with friends or hosting karaoke at your local watering hole.

**LYRX breaks the mold** of traditional, boring and clunky karaoke software. LYRX is **MAC karaoke software** for today’s karaoke enthusiast or budding host.
