## UK's Top 200 DJ Mix of 2014 Unveiled by Renowned DJ Event Planner | Official Playlist

[![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/toprequestedsongs-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/toprequestedsongs-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "toprequestedsongs-coverimage")

A few days back we posted [DJ Intelligence’s compiled list of the USA Mobile DJ top request songs of 2014](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), today we take a look at the UK version posted by [DJ Event Planner](http://djeventplanner.com/mostrequested.htm).

What’s interesting is while there are a couple tracks both countries include at the top of their list (_Pharrell Williams – “Happy” and Journey – “Don’t Stop Believin’”_), that’s where the similar requests end (_at least in the top 10_). It’s clear the UK market’s party-goers have a different taste in tunes, which doesn’t come as a surprise.

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/dex3nowvailable-coverimage.jpg?fit=300%2C169&ssl=1 "dex3nowvailable-coverimage")](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

Regardless of if you’re in the US or the UK, as a Mobile DJ you’ll want to make sure these songs are loading into your [DJ software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) browser.

DJ Event Planner compiled this list based on over 2 million song requests made using the DJ Event Planner song request system. That’s a big sample size, so it’s hard to question it’s accuracy. Here’s the top 10 most requested songs in the UK for the year 2014:

1. **_Killers – Mr. Brightside_**
2. **_Kings Of Leon – Sex On Fire_**
3. **_Black Eyed Peas – I Gotta Feeling_**
4. **_Pharrell Williams – Happy_**
5. **_Bon Jovi Livin’ – On A Prayer_**
6. **_Robin Thicke – Blurred Lines_**
7. **_Whitney Houston – I Wanna Dance With Somebody_**
8. **_Daft Punk – Get Lucky_**
9. **_Journey – Don’t Stop Believin’_**
10. **_Bryan Adams – Summer Of ’69_**

For the entire list of 200 most requested songs in the UK in 2014 click [HERE](http://djeventplanner.com/mostrequesteduk.htm)
