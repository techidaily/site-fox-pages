## **Digital 1 Audio, Inc ([http://www.pcdj.com](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)), a leading designer of performance software for professional DJ’s, karaoke hosts and entertainers, announced the release of PCDJ DEX 2.6\. The new free update for existing PCDJ DEX 2 users includes a myriad of new features including a karaoke singers list and new support for many popular DJ controllers from various manufacturers.**

 Clearwater, Florida (PRWEB) March 19, 2013

 "One of the most highly requested features since we released **PCDJ DEX 2** almost 2 years ago is the inclusion of a **Karaoke Singers List**,” says PCDJ's General Manager, Ryan Sherr. "Our core customer is the do-it-all entertainer, a DJ that aims to offer the total multimedia experience to their clients and patrons. DEX 2.6 introduces a user friendly karaoke singers list, making it a true solution for hosting karaoke shows. We’ve also spent considerable time and effort focusing on the **new video mixing controls**; our users that mix music videos now have various transitions and video effects at their disposal.” Sherr continues, “New DJ controllers seem to come out almost weekly lately, with every major manufacturer in the business offering various models. We’re now up to a total of **50 DJ controllers** supported in **PCDJ DEX 2.6**, including some of the hottest new units. We want our customer’s shows and events to be as memorable as possible, and PCDJ DEX2.6 gives them all the tools to leave a lasting impression.”

 Here are some of the notable features and/or improvements in DEX 2.6: 

* **Karaoke Singers List**
* **New flexible support for HID-only controllers**
* **Better BPM detection and edit function**
* **New video effects and transitions**
* **Ability to increase/decrease font size in the library**
* **New flexible sound card routing for all 4 decks**
* **Improved support for all supported DJ controllers**
* **New DJ controllers supported include:**

* **Numark MixTrack Pro 2**
* **Numark N4**
* **Pioneer DDJ WeGO**
* **Pioneer XDJ Aero**
* **American Audio 10MXR**
* **American Audio 14MXR**
* **American Audio 19MXR**

 PCDJ DEX 2.6’s manufacturer's suggested retail price is $279, but is currently available for only $179 for a limited time (If you own an existing PCDJ software product call for special upgrade pricing)
