## Unlocking Beats On-the-Go: Introducing Beatport's Enhanced Subscription Experience for Professional DJs

[![Beatport LINK Mobile App](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/BP_Mobile_App_KeyArt_02_Final_v2-1024x518-copy.jpg?resize=845%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/BP%5FMobile%5FApp%5FKeyArt%5F02%5FFinal%5Fv2-1024x518-copy.jpg?fit=1030%2C550&ssl=1 "Beatport LINK Mobile App")

[Beatport LINK](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) is baked into [DEX 3 (Pro)](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and provides in-app streaming access to more than nine million tracks. Subscribers to the service can also access curated lists from artists and labels, as well as create their own instantly-ready-in-DEX 3 playlists. It’s the most powerful record collection on the planet and until recently there was a missing component – a Mobile App.

[Beatport](https://www.beatportal.com/news/beatport-new-mobile-app-for-djs-and-refreshed-brand-design/) Wrote:

> _The new mobile app, which is [available today in the Apple AppStore store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/beatport/id1543739988), is the perfect mobile toolset to discover and quickly manage large libraries of music._
> 
> _Placing the power of Beatport LINK in the palm of your hand, the app promises to further empower DJs’ creative workflow and integrate seamlessly into their performance environment by focusing on DJ productivity features like playlist creation, playlist management, and mobile-friendly digging._
> 
> _An Android version of the app will be released later this year, along with additional features for the iOS version._

Available for iOS subscribers now, the mobile app puts millions of tracks in a DJs pocket with unlimited, free streaming.

If you’re a DEX 3 DJ that subscribes to Beatport be sure to [download the app now](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/beatport/id1543739988) and create or select playlists that sync with [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) automatically.
